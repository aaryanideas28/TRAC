## 2. Discrepancy Investigation & Resolution

### Finding
The dashboard displays two distinct metric contexts:
1. **Live Scenario Replay Metrics** (`throughput = 19 trains/hr` / `delay = 3.1 min` / `utilization = 86%`): Generated by the dynamic 6-train corridor simulation in `api_server.py`.
2. **Side-by-Side Impact Benchmark** (`without_ai = 15 trains/hr` / `8.4 min delay` vs `with_ai = 19 trains/hr` / `3.1 min delay`): Derived from the validated 10-train CSMT-Thane benchmark.

### Resolution
- **Can both sets of values legitimately coexist?** **YES.** They represent different operational contexts (`CURRENT REPLAY STATE` vs `VALIDATED 10-TRAIN BENCHMARK`).
- **UI Clarification**: Added explicit visual provenance badges (`CURRENT REPLAY STATE` vs `VALIDATED 10-TRAIN BENCHMARK`) so technical judges can distinguish live simulation states from comparative benchmarks without confusion.

## 3. Canonical Benchmark Definition

- **Dataset & Corridor**: 10 trains (3 Fast Express, 7 Slow Local) on CSMT-Thane Central Line corridor.
- **Topology**: 62 track segments, 86 logical track resources, 60s minimum safety headway buffer.
- **Legacy Single-Resource Baseline**: `200.52 min` total completion delay, `19.55 min` added hold, `66.36 min²` delay variance, `1,172` conflicts.
- **Nexora Infrastructure-Aware CP-SAT**: `195.82 min` total completion delay, `11.65 min` added hold, `61.48 min²` delay variance, `710` conflicts.
- **Net Net Savings**: `-4.7 min` total completion delay (`-2.34%`), `-7.9 min` hold delay (`-40.41%`), `3 → 5` zero-hold trains (`+66.7%`).

| "signal conflict eliminated" | "Modeled Resource Conflict Resolved" | Model evaluates logical track resource occupancy, not physical relay signals. |
